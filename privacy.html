1. Who we are

Baxter is operated by Erik Hope, doing business as Baxter ("Baxter", "we", "us"). You can reach us at privacy@bax.bot.

Baxter is an AI assistant you reach by messaging it. Most people run it on their own hardware, where their data never touches us at all — see Self-hosted installs. This policy describes the service we operate, including the SMS number we run.

2. SMS messaging

Opting in

You opt in to SMS by registering your mobile number with Baxter and confirming it, or by texting our number first. We only message numbers that have opted in. We do not buy, rent, or import phone number lists, and we never send marketing or promotional text messages.

What we send

Two kinds of message, both of them things you asked for: replies to what you text Baxter, and the alerts and reminders you set up — a price you're watching, a deadline you asked to be nudged about.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Baxter replies when you message it and sends alerts or reminders when something you asked it to watch actually happens, so the number of messages depends entirely on how you use it. There is no fixed schedule and no recurring broadcast.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Baxter does not charge you for text messages, but your mobile carrier may. Check your plan — rates and any per-message charges are between you and your carrier.

Stopping messages, and getting help

Text STOP to our number at any time to stop all messages. We will send one confirmation and then nothing further. Text START to opt back in, or HELP for support information. You can also email privacy@bax.bot. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your mobile number is not shared

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

In plain terms: we do not sell your phone number, we do not rent it, and we do not hand it to advertisers, data brokers, affiliates, or anyone else who wants to market to you. Your opt-in to SMS stays with us. The only parties who ever touch your number are the messaging carrier that has to route the text and, where applicable, a support vendor acting for us under contract — both covered in Who we share it with.

3. What we collect

Mobile number
The number you register or message us from, plus the date, time, and method of your opt-in and any later opt-out. We keep the consent record because we have to be able to show that you agreed.
Message content
What you text Baxter and what it texts back. This is the substance of your request, so Baxter needs it to answer.
Message metadata
Timestamps, delivery status, and error codes returned by the carrier.
What you ask Baxter to remember
Notes, reminders, watch requests, and project documents you create by asking for them. Baxter is built to keep these across conversations — that is the feature, and it means the content persists until you delete it.
Website analytics
Aggregate pageview counts, with no cookies and no cross-site tracking. See This website.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, or any account credentials. Baxter has no permission to log in to anything of yours, by design.

4. How we use it

We use what we collect only to:

  • Answer you, and send the alerts and reminders you asked for.
  • Keep the notes and documents you asked Baxter to keep.
  • Prove your consent to receive messages, and honor opt-outs.
  • Diagnose delivery failures and keep the service running.
  • Prevent abuse of the number, and comply with law.

We do not use your messages or your number to advertise to you, and we do not sell your information. We do not use your message content to train AI models.

5. Who we share it with

We share information with a small set of providers who process it on our behalf, only to the extent they need it to make the service work, and only under agreements that bar them from using it for their own purposes.

Messaging carrier
Text messages physically cannot be delivered without one. Our SMS provider and the mobile networks receive your number and message content in order to route the message to your handset.
AI model provider
Baxter sends the content of your request to the AI model that generates the reply. Your phone number is not sent with it. Model providers we use are under terms that prohibit training on the content we send.
Infrastructure
Hosting and error monitoring for the servers Baxter runs on.
Legal
If we are legally compelled by valid process, or where disclosure is necessary to protect someone's safety or our rights.

To restate the point in §2, because it is the one that matters most: no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, and text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are used in US state privacy laws.

6. How long we keep it

Message content and the notes Baxter keeps stay until you delete them or ask us to. Delivery metadata is kept for up to 12 months for troubleshooting. Records of your opt-in and opt-out are kept for as long as we operate the messaging service, and for at least four years after, because messaging regulations require us to be able to evidence consent. If you opt out, we retain your number on a suppression list — that is what guarantees we don't message you again.

7. Your choices

  • Stop messages. Text STOP. It takes effect immediately.
  • See what we have. Email us and we'll send you a copy.
  • Delete it. Email us and we'll delete your data, except the consent and suppression records described above.
  • Correct it. Tell us what's wrong and we'll fix it.

Depending on where you live, you may have these rights under law — including under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR. We extend them to everyone regardless. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them. Write to privacy@bax.bot and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the systems holding message content is limited to those who need it to operate the service. Baxter's own runtime is deliberately constrained — it runs in a container, confined to its own workspace, and by default cannot read the credentials it runs under. No system is perfectly secure, and SMS in particular is not an encrypted channel: treat text messages as you would a postcard, and don't send Baxter anything you would not want a carrier to hold.

9. This website

bax.bot uses GoatCounter for analytics. It sets no cookies, collects no personal data, and does not track you across sites — it counts pageviews. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of the request, as any server does. There are no advertising or social tracking scripts on this site.

10. Self-hosted installs

Baxter is open-source software that most people run themselves. If you installed Baxter on your own machine, your conversations, notes, and API keys stay on your hardware and we never see them — there is no account, no middleman, and nothing reported back to us. This policy does not describe that data, because we do not have it. If you connect your own self-hosted Baxter to your own messaging account, you are the operator of that service and responsible for its privacy practices and registrations, not us.

11. Children

Baxter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. For changes that materially affect how we handle your information, we'll notify you by text or email before they take effect.
